Why we learn Web Development?

High Demand

Almost every business needs a website or an app to operate and to stay relevant without being outcompeted by other markets.

Creative and Practical

You can build your own ideas from scratch.

Career Opportunities:

Frontend Developer

A developer specializes in the visual and interactive part of the website.

Backend Developer

A developer specializes in the logic or processing happening behind the website.

FullStack Developer

A developer specializes in both the visual/interactive part and the logic/processing part of the website.

Did you know?

Most websites you use daily are built in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
