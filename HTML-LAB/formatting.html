Here are some of the good to know tags that will help you in your journey:

1. The Bold tag is used to make the text bold.

2. The Italic tag is used to make the text italic.

3. The Underline tag is used to underline the text.

4. The Strong tag is used to indicate strong importance.

5. The Emphasis tag is used to emphasize the text.

6. The Mark tag is used to highlight the text.

7. The Small tag is used to make the text small.

8. The Subscript tag is used to make the text subscript.

9. The Superscript tag is used to make the text superscript.

10. The Delete tag is used to strike through the text.

11. The Insert tag is used to insert text.
